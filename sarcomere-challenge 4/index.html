            🔑 Examiner view · Answer key

            How to play

            1. Round 1 · Sequence Sort: drag the 12 steps of contraction into order. Right cards snap and glow, wrong ones shake. Hints cost 50 points.
            2. Round 2 · Build: assemble a relaxed sarcomere from the pieces (watch out for decoys), check it, then label the A band, I band, H zone and M line.
            3. Round 3 · What's Missing? each scenario removes one ingredient (like ATP or Ca²⁺). Tap which step fails, then what the muscle does. Wrong answers get a nudge, and after 2 wrong tries you can buy a hint.
            4. Quick-fire: fast multiple-choice questions with explanations.

            🔥 Streaks of 3+ correct answers earn bonus points. 👥 Partner Mode gives the examiner a checklist of things the player should explain out loud.
